## 中文

### Markdown 笔记
- 引用的笔记目录可以转为导入：在侧栏这一段的标题上右键选「转为导入…」，整个目录会复制进工作区的 Notes，然后取消引用；原目录不会被改动。开着的笔记会跟到新位置。
- 导入笔记目录时，如果选的是 Obsidian 库里的子目录，笔记里用到的、放在这个目录外面的图片也会一起复制进来。
- 导入时 Notes 下已有同名目录，会合并进去，不再另建一个；内容不同的旧文件先放进废纸篓再替换。
- 点击编辑区顶上的标题就能直接给笔记改名。
- 侧栏会列出笔记目录里的图片，单击或按空格用快速查看打开。
- 公式里的 `\lt`、`\gt` 现在能正常显示。

### 界面
- 关闭标签后，其余标签先保持宽度，等鼠标离开标签栏再重新排开，方便连续关闭；标题显示不全时，鼠标停在上面会显示全名。

## English

### Markdown notes
- A referenced notes folder can now be converted to imported notes: right-click its section header in the sidebar and choose "Convert to Imported…". The whole folder is copied into the workspace's Notes and the reference is removed; the original folder is not touched. Open notes follow to the new location.
- When importing a folder inside an Obsidian vault, images the notes use from elsewhere in the vault are copied along.
- Importing into Notes where a folder with the same name already exists now merges into it instead of creating a second folder; old files with different content are moved to the Trash before being replaced.
- Click the title at the top of the editor to rename a note.
- The sidebar lists images in note folders; click or press Space to open them in Quick Look.
- `\lt` and `\gt` in formulas now display correctly.

### Interface
- After closing a tab, the other tabs keep their width until the pointer leaves the tab bar, so you can close several in a row; hovering a truncated title shows its full name.
