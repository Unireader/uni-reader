## 中文

### ⚠️ 请把所有设备都更新
这一版改了笔迹的保存格式。用新版打开工作区时，会在后台把已有的笔迹转换成新格式；转换之后，**旧版 UniReader（0.2.6 及更早，也包括没更新的安卓 App）打开这个工作区，笔迹会显示为空**。请在所有 Mac 和平板上都更新后再使用。

### 新功能和改进
- 笔迹改用更紧凑的格式保存：笔迹多的文档和画板打开明显更快，工作区文件也更小。PDF 上的笔迹、草稿纸和画板都包括在内。
- 平板连接 Mac 使用时，可以在草稿纸和画板上框选笔迹：圈选、拖动移动、拖角或边上的手柄缩放，也能剪切、复制、粘贴。
- 新建画板的窗口重新排版；页面大小除了 A4 / A5 / Letter / 当前屏幕，也可以直接输入宽和高。
- 分页画板的「页面设置」面板改为底部切换「本页 / 所有页」，默认是本页：本页可以改背景、在前后插入一页、删除这一页；所有页可以改页面大小和批量设置背景。按钮图标换成了更好认的一个。
- 分页画板的「纸样」只保留纸色（每页的背景在「页面设置」里改）。

## English

### ⚠️ Please update every device
This version changes how strokes are stored. When you open a workspace, existing strokes are converted to the new format in the background. After that, **older versions of UniReader (0.2.6 and earlier, including an out-of-date Android app) will show the workspace's strokes as empty**. Update UniReader on every Mac and tablet before using it.

### New features & improvements
- Strokes are stored in a more compact format: documents and boards with lots of handwriting open noticeably faster, and workspaces take less space. This covers ink on PDFs, scratch pads and boards.
- When the tablet is connected to the Mac, you can lasso strokes on scratch pads and boards: circle to select, drag to move, drag a corner or edge handle to resize, and cut, copy or paste.
- The New Board window has a cleaner layout, and besides A4 / A5 / Letter / current screen you can type a page width and height.
- The Page Settings panel for paged boards now switches between This Page and All Pages at the bottom, starting on This Page: change the background, insert a page before or after, or delete the page; All Pages covers page size and backgrounds for several pages at once. Its toolbar icon is easier to recognize.
- For paged boards, Paper now only sets the paper color (page backgrounds live in Page Settings).
