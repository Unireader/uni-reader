## 中文

### 习题
- 习题可以不关联 PDF：让 Agent 把它自己出的题、别处找来的题或你给它的题存进一个分组，不用先有一篇 PDF。分组在侧栏「习题」里和书并列，每个分组一行；错题本和收藏底下也能按分组看。这种题没有页码和原题图。
- 分组可以改名：在侧栏的分组上右键「重命名分组…」；改成另一个已有分组的名字，两组就合在一起。
- 在习题列表里选中不关联 PDF 的题，右键「移动到分组」可以换分组、新建分组或移出分组。
- 已有的题可以和 PDF 分开：在习题列表里右键「解除与 PDF 的关联…」。题目、做题记录、错题本、收藏、笔记和标签都保留，题会移到一个以书名命名的分组里；页码和原题区域会去掉。
- 删除一篇 PDF 时，它的习题不再跟着删除：会移到以书名命名的分组里，做题记录都在。

### 升级须知
- 这一版的数据版本升到 28。连接服务器的工作区，服务器和各个设备要一起升级：服务器 0.7.0、安卓版 0.3.23。只升了一边时，没升的那一边只能下载、不能上传。

## English

### Exercises
- Exercises no longer have to belong to a PDF: ask the Agent to save questions it wrote, found elsewhere or got from you into a group, with no PDF needed. Groups appear in the Exercises part of the sidebar next to the books, one row each; Mistakes and Starred can be browsed by group too. These questions have no page number and no original image.
- Groups can be renamed: right-click a group in the sidebar and choose Rename Group…. Giving it the name of another group merges the two.
- In the exercise list, select questions that aren't linked to a PDF and right-click Move to Group to move them to another group, a new group, or out of their group.
- Existing questions can be unlinked from their PDF: right-click them in the exercise list and choose Unlink from PDF…. The questions, your answers, mistakes, stars, notes and tags stay, and the questions move to a group named after the book; the page number and the question area are dropped.
- Deleting a PDF no longer deletes its exercises: they move to a group named after the book, with your practice history intact.

### Before you update
- The data version is now 28. For workspaces on a server, update the server and all your devices together: server 0.7.0 and the Android app 0.3.23. If only one side is updated, the side that wasn't can download but can't upload.
