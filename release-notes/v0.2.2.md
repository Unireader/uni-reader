## 中文

只修一个崩溃，建议装上。

### 修复
- 鼠标滚轮滚到展开的文字笔记气泡上时，App 会立刻崩溃退出。内容比较短、气泡里本来就不需要滚动的那些笔记必然触发；内容很长、气泡自己要滚的那些不受影响。图片笔记的气泡同样修正。

## English

A crash fix only — worth installing.

### Fixes
- Scrolling the mouse wheel over an expanded text note bubble crashed the app immediately. It happened every time with shorter notes, where the bubble has nothing to scroll; longer notes that scroll inside the bubble were unaffected. Image note bubbles are fixed too.
