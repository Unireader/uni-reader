## 中文

### 新功能：选择题也能手写演算
- 做选择题时，选项下面多了一个「手写」开关。打开后是一块小画板，和填空题、主观题用的是同一块：笔、橡皮、撤销、清除、加高。
- 先在画板上演算，再点选项（多选题点「提交」）。演算会跟着这次作答一起保存，判题仍然只看你选的选项。
- 以后翻回这道题，上次的演算显示在选项下面。点「再做一次」画板会清空。
- 写了一半翻到别的题，回来时演算还在。
- 这个开关和填空题、主观题的「手写」分开记：选择题开着不会让填空题也摊开画板，反过来也一样。

## English

### New: Handwritten working for multiple-choice questions
- Multiple-choice questions now have a Handwriting switch below the options. Turning it on opens the same small pad used for fill-in-the-blank and free-response questions: pen, eraser, Undo, Clear and More Space.
- Work the problem out on the pad, then click an option (or Submit for multiple-answer questions). Your working is saved with that attempt; the answer is still judged only by the options you chose.
- When you come back to the question later, the working from last time is shown below the options. Try Again clears the pad.
- If you move to another question halfway through, your working is still there when you return.
- This switch is remembered separately from Handwriting on fill-in-the-blank and free-response questions, so turning one on doesn't open the pad on the other kind.
