## 中文

本版汇总 0.1.38 以来的改动。

### 搜索
- 切换到上一个/下一个命中时会有滚动动画飞到目标位置，命中会显示在阅读区中间，不再贴顶被工具栏或查找条挡住。
- 命中切换的瞬间会短暂闪烁提示；可以在「设置 › 阅读」里关闭这个动画，只保留常态高亮。
- 新一轮搜索不再固定跳到文档里最靠前的那个命中，而是跳到离当前阅读位置最近的一个。
- 扫描件（OCR）搜索现在只框住命中的那段文字，不再整行高亮；命中框圆角也更明显。

### 复制链接
- 阅读区右键新增「复制链接」：在页面空白处、书签、文字笔记（图钉和展开的卡片）、草稿纸图钉上都可以复制一个 `unireader://` 链接，粘贴到别处点开会跳回这个位置；右键点在一条高亮上会精确链到那条高亮。

### 修复与改进
- 修复 MCP 服务部分工具返回结果里可为空的字段（如目录页码、文档 id）被严格校验的客户端拒收的问题。

### 已知问题
- 复制草稿纸图钉的链接目前只能跳到对应的页面位置，还不会自动打开那张草稿纸。

## English

This release covers changes since 0.1.38.

### Search
- Switching to the previous/next match now animates the scroll to the target, and the match lands in the middle of the reading area instead of being tucked under the toolbar or find bar.
- The active match briefly flashes when you switch to it; turn this off in Settings › Reading if you only want the plain highlight.
- Starting a new search no longer always jumps to the very first match in the document — it jumps to whichever match is closest to where you're currently reading.
- Searching scanned (OCR) pages now highlights only the matched text, not the whole line; the highlight's corners are also more rounded.

### Copy Link
- The reader's context menu adds "Copy Link": on a blank page area, a bookmark, a text note (pin or expanded card), or a scratchpad pin, it copies a `unireader://` link that jumps back to that spot when opened. Right-clicking directly on a highlight links to that specific highlight.

### Fixes and Improvements
- Fixed MCP tool results with nullable fields (such as a table-of-contents page or a document id) being rejected by strictly-validating clients.

### Known Issues
- Copying a scratchpad pin's link currently jumps to that page position but does not yet open the scratchpad itself.
