## 中文

### 习题
- 可以清除做题记录：练习列表里选几道题右键「清除做题记录…」，或者在侧栏右键一组题（全部 / 错题本 / 收藏 / 某本书 / 某一章）。错题本跟着更新；从书上导入的作答会留着。清掉的记录先放进回收站，可以恢复。

### 平板（连接 Mac）
- 平板上能看到高亮了：铺色、下划线、方框、删除线四种画法，以及批注的底色，和 Mac 上一样。需要平板也更新到新版。
- Mac 正在看 Markdown 笔记时，平板上能直接读到这篇笔记（只读）；平板的标签栏上显示笔记名，不再是「未命名」。
- 修正：Mac 停在习题、画板或笔记标签时连上平板，切回 PDF 后平板一直白屏；平板有时停在第一页，要在 Mac 上点一下那个标签才跳过去。

### 其他
- 标签栏：标签平分宽度、放不下时自动变窄；鼠标停在标签上显示完整名字；「+」按钮不再一直带着蓝色焦点框。
- 笔记里的分步演示新增两个套件：定点数乘法（原码一位乘、补码一位乘）和定点数除法（恢复余数法、加减交替法），按教材的运算表格一步步画出来。

## English

### Exercises
- You can now clear practice records: select some questions in the practice list and right-click Clear Practice Record…, or right-click a group in the sidebar (All, Mistakes, Starred, a book or a chapter). The Mistakes list is updated to match; answers imported from the book stay. Cleared records go to Recently Deleted first, so you can put them back.

### Tablet (connected to the Mac)
- Highlights now show on the tablet — fill, underline, box and strikethrough — along with the color behind annotations, just as on the Mac. The tablet needs the new version too.
- When the Mac is showing a Markdown note, the tablet can read that note (read-only), and its tab on the tablet shows the note's name instead of "Untitled".
- Fixed: connecting the tablet while the Mac was on an exercise, board or note tab left the tablet blank after switching back to a PDF; the tablet sometimes stayed on the first page until you clicked that tab on the Mac.

### Also
- Tab bar: tabs share the width evenly and shrink when space runs out; hovering a tab shows its full name; the + button no longer keeps a blue focus ring.
- Two more kits for step-by-step demos in notes: fixed-point multiplication (sign-magnitude and Booth) and fixed-point division (restoring and non-restoring), drawn step by step like the textbook tables.
