## 中文

本版汇总 0.1.40 以来的改动。

### 新建标签
- 标签栏「+」和 ⌘T 不再新开一个空标签，而是弹出选文档窗口：本工作区书库里的文档按最近打开排序，可以直接输入关键词按标题或分组过滤，方向键/回车挑选打开；已打开的文档会标注「已打开」，点击后直接切过去。
- 标签栏没显示时（只有一篇文档，或草稿纸开着）⌘T 同样会在阅读区底部弹出这个窗口。

### 标签排横向滚动
- 标签太多排不下、标签排出现横向滚动时，鼠标悬停在上面直接用滚轮就能滚，不用再按住 Shift。

### 修复与改进
- 修复接鼠标使用系统的传统滚动条时，底部标签栏与滚动条互相挤占、底边留出一条空槽的显示问题。

## English

This release covers changes since 0.1.40.

### New Tab
- The tab bar's "+" and ⌘T no longer open a blank tab — they open a document picker instead: documents in this workspace are listed by most recently opened, type to filter by title or group, use the arrow keys or Return to pick one, and already-open documents are marked "Already Open" and just switch you to that tab.
- When the tab bar itself is hidden (only one document open, or the scratchpad is showing), ⌘T still opens this picker at the bottom of the reading area.

### Tab Strip Scrolling
- When there are too many tabs to fit and the tab strip scrolls horizontally, just hover over it and scroll — no need to hold Shift anymore.

### Fixes and Improvements
- Fixed the bottom tab bar and scrollbar crowding each other when using a mouse with the system's legacy scrollbar style, which left an empty gap along the bottom edge.
