## 中文

### 新功能
- 习题：让 Agent 识别 PDF 里的选择题（单选、多选），导入工作区后在 App 里练习、记错题、收藏。
  - 导入：对 Agent 说「识别第 30~35 页的习题」这类话即可，App 自带的 Agent 面板和通过 MCP 连接的外部 Agent 都能做。答案印在书后的，翻到答案页再让 Agent 补上答案和解析。你在书上已经做过的题（高亮或手写），Agent 也能一起认出来导入。仓库附带一份给 Agent 看的操作说明（`skills/unireader-exercise-import`）。
  - 侧栏多了「习题」一段（工作区里有题才出现）：全部习题、错题本、收藏、每本有题的书，行尾是题数。书可以按章节展开；错题本和收藏也按书、再按章节展开。章节默认按题目所在页码从 PDF 目录里找，也可以在列表里右键「设置章节…」手动改。
  - 点哪一组都在同一个习题标签页里换；想同时开着几组，右键选「在新标签页中打开」。错题本另用一个标签页。
  - 练习：一次一题。单选点一下就判，多选勾完点「提交」；键盘 A~H 选、Return 提交、←/→ 翻题。判完显示对错、正确答案和解析；答错的题自动进错题本，只能手动「移出错题本」。还可以看原题截图、点「到 PDF 原页」跳回书上那一页。
  - 做过的题直接显示上次的作答和对错，以及一共做了几次、对了几次。想重答点「再做一次」；顶上的「全部重做」把这一组题都重做一遍，列表里多选后右键也能重做选中的几道。重做不会删掉以前的作答记录。
  - 顶上的「只看没做过」只显示还没做过的题（错题本里没有这个开关）。
  - 列表：一行一题，能看到章节、页码、题型、你的答案和作答次数；双击从这题开始练。删掉的题可以从「回收站」里恢复；删除文档时，它的习题也一起进回收站。
  - 离线镜像同步时，题目和作答也一起同步。
- 平板上也能做题：安卓平板连接 Mac 时，左侧抽屉多一页「习题」，平板可以自己开一组题，Mac 不跟着换；两边开着同一组题时，作答和翻到第几题都同步。平板直接打开工作区时也能做题。**平板需要更新到安卓版 0.3.10 或更新**，下载：https://github.com/Unireader/uni-reader-android/releases/tag/2026-10-11-1

## English

### New
- Exercises: have the Agent recognize the multiple-choice questions (single and multiple answer) in a PDF and import them into the workspace, then practice them in the app, keep track of the ones you got wrong, and star the ones you want to keep.
  - Importing: just ask the Agent something like "recognize the exercises on pages 30 to 35". Both the built-in Agent panel and external agents connected over MCP can do it. If the answers are printed at the back of the book, go to the answer pages and ask the Agent to add the answers and explanations. Questions you already answered in the book (highlighted or handwritten) can be recognized and imported too. The repository includes instructions for agents (`skills/unireader-exercise-import`).
  - The sidebar has a new "Exercises" section (shown only when the workspace has exercises): All Exercises, Mistakes, Starred, and one row per book with exercises, each with a question count. Books open up by chapter; Mistakes and Starred also open up by book, then by chapter. Chapters are looked up in the PDF's table of contents from each question's page; you can also change them with right-click › "Set Chapter…" in the list.
  - Every set opens in the same exercise tab; to keep several sets open at once, right-click and choose "Open in New Tab". Mistakes use a tab of their own.
  - Practice: one question at a time. A single-choice question is checked as soon as you click an option; for a multiple-choice question, tick your options and click "Submit". On the keyboard, A–H pick options, Return submits, and ←/→ move between questions. After checking you see whether you were right, the correct answer, and the explanation. Questions you get wrong go into Mistakes automatically and only leave when you click "Remove from Mistakes". You can also view the original question as an image, or click "Open Page" to go back to that page in the book.
  - Questions you have done before show your last answer, whether it was right, and how many times you have done it and got it right. To answer again, click "Try Again"; "Redo All Questions" at the top redoes the whole set, and in the list you can select several questions and redo them from the right-click menu. Redoing never deletes your earlier answers.
  - "Not Done Only" at the top shows only the questions you haven't done yet (it is not shown in Mistakes).
  - List: one question per row, with its chapter, page, type, your answer, and how many times you have done it; double-click to start practicing from that question. Deleted exercises can be restored from Recently Deleted; deleting a document moves its exercises to Recently Deleted along with it.
  - Syncing with an offline mirror brings the exercises and your answers along.
- Practice on the tablet too: when an Android tablet is connected to the Mac, its left drawer has a new "Exercises" page. The tablet can open its own set of questions without changing what the Mac shows; when both have the same set open, answers and the current question stay in sync. A tablet that opens a workspace directly can practice as well. **The tablet needs Android app 0.3.10 or later**; download: https://github.com/Unireader/uni-reader-android/releases/tag/2026-10-11-1

### Fixes
- After a PDF file was changed outside the app (for example, its table of contents was edited), the "File Changed" prompt kept coming back after you clicked "Link as New Version" or "Open Anyway".
